    How it works

    1. Start anywhere: tap a bar and the band starts from there.
    2. Loop a tricky spot: press and hold its first bar, slide to its last bar and let go (on a computer, drag across the bars). Tap the round loop button to go back to the whole tune.
    3. Repeats: the × button sets how many times the band plays through.
    4. Build speed: start slow, and switch on Speed up +5 each time to get a little faster every time through.
    5. Hear it plays the written notes along with the band.
