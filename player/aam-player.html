    How to practise with the band

    1. Start slow (♩ = 80–100). Play Section 1, then Section 3, with the band. Switch on Hear it played first if you want to hear how it goes. Pianists: switch off Piano chords when you comp yourself.
    2. Stuck on a spot? Choose Loop bars, tap the first and last bar of the tricky part, and set how many times to play it.
    3. Switch on Speed up +5 each time: the band gets a little faster every time through, up to the goal tempo.
    4. Second time through: improvise, using the arpeggios, scales, patterns and licks from your PDF.
